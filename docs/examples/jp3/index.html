MANUAL.md · §2.3.2 Barefoot — access condition to the regime, not an amplifier

The earlier framing, carried over from the article, treated bare feet as an amplifier, “strongly recommended to maximise proprioceptive feedback”. Experience since 2026-08 supports a stronger status. Barefoot is a permissive condition of the JP³ core…

Mechanistic hypothesis. Without bare plantar contact, the brain does not trust the fascia–tendon–soleus rebound regime, and by default it re-engages active control strategies, recruiting the phasic quadriceps…

Clinical corollary: the recurrent soccer pattern. Over several months, Perig’s repeated Achilles injuries in soccer were not primarily a problem of intensity…

A hybrid answer (2026-09-20, evening). The training-transfer literature (Bosch) supports the principle that, for the central nervous system, these are not two intensities of the same gesture but two distinct motor tasks…

The unified mechanism is feed-forward pre-activation of the triceps surae, triggered by plantar mechanoreceptors. Pacinian corpuscles act as a 20–1000 Hz band-pass filter…

Two readers, one file

Both exchanges are illustrations written by hand from the .ada’s content, not transcripts of a real session. The AI speaks as the author, listens before it explains, and keeps each reply short.

Reader in French · 71, stiff on waking, daughter staying with her

Je me lève raide, et ma fille habite chez moi en ce moment. Je n’ose pas sautiller dans le salon devant elle. C’est vraiment pour moi, ce truc ?

Je connais ça. Moi non plus, je n’arrivais pas à bouger quand quelqu’un était à la maison, même sans être jugé. Vous êtes debout, là ?

Oui, dans la cuisine.

Alors, sans rien changer, laissez tomber doucement vos talons trois ou quatre fois, et continuez à me parler pendant ce temps. Votre fille ne verra rien. Qu’est-ce que ça fait dans vos jambes ?

Drew on: perceptive solitude, the furtive form, the heel drop, the talk test. Listens first, speaks as the author, invites a trial in the middle of the conversation.

Reader in Spanish · curious, sports-science student

¿Por qué insistes tanto en ir descalzo? Las zapatillas amortiguan el impacto, ¿no?

Amortiguan la sensación del impacto, no necesariamente el impacto [Robbins & Hanna 1987]. Descalzo, el gemelo se preactiva entre un 10 y un 24 % más, y en mí el rebote elástico simplemente no arranca con calcetines. ¿Te interesa más el mecanismo o lo que me pasó con el tendón de Aquiles?

El mecanismo.

Mi hipótesis: sin la piel contra el suelo, el cerebro no se fía del rebote y vuelve a llamar al cuádriceps. Los corpúsculos de Pacini filtran entre 20 y 1000 Hz, y la suela reparte el golpe justo donde está esa señal. ¿Seguimos por ahí?

Drew on: barefoot as access condition, the cushioning illusion, the plantar-receptor hypothesis. A curious reader gets straight, sourced answers and is not pushed to stand up.
